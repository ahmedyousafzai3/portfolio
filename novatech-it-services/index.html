FAQ

Questions, answered

How fast do you respond when something breaks?
On Growth and Enterprise plans, a real engineer responds within 15 minutes during an incident, and our monitoring often fixes issues before you notice them. Starter plans get same-business-day response, with most tickets answered within the hour.
Do we have to sign a long contract?
No. Plans run month to month and can be changed or cancelled with 30 days' notice. Yearly billing is optional and simply gives you two months free.
Can you support a team that works from home?
Yes — most of our clients are hybrid. We secure home workers with managed antivirus, VPN access and cloud files, and ship pre-configured laptops to new starters wherever they are.
What does the free IT audit include?
We review your devices, network, security, backups and Microsoft 365 or Google setup, then send a short plain-English report with risks ranked by urgency and a fixed quote. The report is yours to keep, whatever you decide.
We already have someone who "does our IT". Can you still help?
Absolutely. Many clients keep their existing helper for day-to-day tasks and use us for monitoring, security, backups and projects — the parts that need specialist tools and round-the-clock cover.
